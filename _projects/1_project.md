## Overview

During my internship, I worked on a real-time 3D vision motion-capture system. The project reconstructs human motion from multi-view camera observations and streams the result for downstream visualization and applications.

## Highlights

- Designed and integrated the real-time 3D vision motion-capture pipeline.
- Worked on multi-camera data processing, pose estimation, and 3D reconstruction.
- Focused on stable, low-latency output suitable for live demonstrations.
